## Procedural 3D authoring verification & static review resolutions — 2026-10-17

Read sections 9–18 of [3D model authoring enhancements](3D-MODEL-AUTHORING-ENHANCEMENTS.md) for the verified capabilities and procedural architecture of the kit. The authoring pipeline in `tools/RogueModelKit.py` and `tools/generate_unit_model.py` (mirrored in `.agents/skills/rogue-command-3d-model-authoring/`) provides:
1. **Subsystem Geometry Budget Profiling (`profile_model_budget_and_drawcalls`)**: Calculates true triangle counts and profiles distribution across 5 functional subsystems (Chassis & Hull, Turret & Cabin, Mobility & Wheels, Weapons, Optics & Greebles) with draw call predictions and prefix-aware name matching.
2. **Multi-Level LOD Generation (`generate_lod_chain`)**: Automated decimation to LOD0 (100%), LOD1 (50%), and LOD2 (22–25%) preserving planar boundaries and material boundaries (`delimit={"NORMAL", "MATERIAL"}`), exporting companion `<Name>_LODs.fbx`.
3. **Physics Collision Hulls, Hardpoints & Unity Collider Conversion (`generate_collision_hulls` & `VehiclePrefabBuilder.cs`)**: Flat-shaded low-poly box colliders (`<Prefix>_COLLIDER_Hull`, `<Prefix>_COLLIDER_Turret`) become approximate Unity `BoxCollider` shapes. The builder disables their visible renderers and avoids adding a duplicate metadata collider. Detached suffixes `era`, `aps`, `sponson`, and `fuel_drums` are normalized to their hardpoint names; their meshes are excluded from the primary FBX, atlas, LODs, and base hull bounds. If tracked generated hulls exist before export, they are replaced using the filtered base mesh set. When the matching hardpoint socket exists, Unity parents the detached FBX under an identity root-level `RCL_AttachmentGroup_<Type>`; if absent, the builder warns and falls back to the visual root. In either path it preserves the imported FBX root's local TRS and retains its source material. `RuntimeVisualDirector` toggles a generated attachment group while the physical socket remains in place. Separate-group ERA and fuel-drum VFX stay parented to the active physical socket; legacy prefabs that use the socket as the visual emit VFX under the active entity root at the captured socket world pose. Unity import/prefab construction for detached accessories remains unverified.
4. **Multi-Channel Procedural Vertex Shading (`bake_advanced_vertex_shading`)**: Encodes 4 properties into RGBA vertex colors (`Mesh.colors32`): R = Cavity AO/dirt, G = Curvature edge wear, B = Thermal soot on exhausts/muzzles, A = Team tint mask. Surface wear levels `pristine`, `field_tested`, and `battle_hardened` control intensity.
5. **Canonical Gameplay Socket Hierarchy (`create_unity_locator_sockets`)**: Exports empty locators oriented to Unity conventions (+Z forward, +Y up). Besides the original muzzle, turret, recoil, sensor, exhaust, damage, center-of-mass, and ground-contact sockets, animated antennae and VTOL units use the exact unprefixed names `Socket_Antenna`, `Socket_ThrusterVector_L`, and `Socket_ThrusterVector_R`. `Socket_Antenna` is the parent pivot for all visible antenna meshes; runtime lookup must not substitute a `Sensor_*` mesh or locator.
6. **Companion Gameplay Metadata JSON (`export_gameplay_metadata`)**: Generates sidecar `<Name>.gameplay.json` declaring `visualRootPath` for native templates (`Vehicle` vs `GameObject/BombBike`), `BoxCollider` bounds, kinematics, and damage particle thresholds.
7. **Procedural Burned Wreckage State (`make_wreckage_variant`)**: Exports `<Name>_Wreckage.fbx` and `.blend` with dislodged/tilted turret, charred armor materials, and severe vertex damage.
8. **19 Tactical Archetypes & 9 Weapons**: Full procedural matrix spanning ground, hover, walker, airborne, and super-heavy platforms (`flak_anti_air`, `siege_crawler`, `drone_carrier`, `quad_mech`, `combat_harvester`, etc.) armed with railguns, quad flak autocannons, plasma mortars, cannons, gatlings, missile pods, and howitzers.
9. **Headless CLI Generator Suite**: `tools/generate_unit_model.py` supports 14 presets, including the `orbital_defense` strategic-structure route, subsystem toggles (`--outriggers`, `--flight-deck`, `--excavator-drum`), direct hex palette overrides (`--primary-color`, `--light-armor-color`, `--emissive-color`), `--dry-run` planning, `--batch-all-archetypes` sequential generation, and `--report-json` structural diagnostics.
10. **PBR Texture Atlas Baking & 1-Draw-Call Optimization (`bake_pbr_texture_atlas`, `--bake-atlas`)**: True PBR dual-pass baking (Albedo, Emission, Normal, Roughness, Metallic via emission rerouting). Packs Metallic and Smoothness ($1.0 - \text{Roughness}$) into `<Prefix>_MetallicGloss.png` following Unity Standard Shader specs, with viewport separate/invert color nodes.
11. **Skeletal Armature Rigging & Automated Unity Animator Setup (`VehiclePrefabBuilder.cs`)**: Hierarchical bone armature with rigid vertex group binding and baked NLA action clips (`Action_Idle`, `Action_Drive`, `Action_FireRecoil`, `Action_Death`). For animated primary FBXs the builder preserves the imported Avatar, loops Idle/Drive, and wires movement, fire, and death transitions; `RuntimeVisualDirector` drives them from entity motion, native shot time, and death state. Static implementation only; Unity import and Steam playback remain unverified.
12. **Procedural VFX & Energy Shields (`--vfx-flash`, `--vfx-thruster`, `--shield-bubble`)**: Star-spiked muzzle flash meshes, conical thruster plumes, and geodesic shield bubble envelopes (`_ShieldBubble` excluded from ground clearance limits).
13. **Dynamic In-Game Combat Lifecycle (`RuntimeVisualDirector.cs`)**: Modular ERA detonation at $\le 75\%$ HP; damaged variant activation or procedural soot scorch (`MaterialPropertyBlock`) + fuel drum jettison at $\le 50\%$ HP; fire particle spawning, $+4.5^\circ$ jammed turret tilt, and APS burnout at $\le 25\%$ HP. Fully reversible on health recovery. Radar angular speed is 30 RPM = 180 degrees/second. Turret targets must be active in the hierarchy and pass the exact `EntityController.IsEnemy(EntityController)` check; failure is closed. These remain static implementation findings pending Steam animation/targeting evidence.
14. **3-Point RTS Studio Lighting & Calibrated Elevation (`render_tactical_icons`, `--icon-elevation`)**: Calibrated 3-point studio rig (Key Sun 4.2, Fill Sun 1.8, Rim Sun 2.4) and parameterized exact elevation (default $45.0^\circ$). The current UV-island margin is `0.02`. Blender-side authoring changes have static review; the new Unity prefab-builder/runtime animation changes still need Unity import and Steam playback checks. The latest offline harness result is 109/110 checks passed, with Windows symlink validation skipped because the environment lacks the required privilege.

`ValidateStageGenerationCompatibility` runs before a pack commit. It validates the union of previously loaded and candidate stages because AI from a later pack is appended to each progression's global AI list and can invalidate an earlier stage's map-size interval. Candidate selection follows AI `restrictedToMaps`, then World `restrictedToMaps`, then progression maps. Since `LevelProgression.CreateLevelMap` samples explicit restrictions without reapplying activation/demo filters, every in-range AI/World restricted map must be active and demo-allowed when applicable. If a selected map's `restrictedToWorlds` restriction applies, every entry must be non-null and active, and demo generation requires every entry to be demo-allowed; the game may choose any World from that list. Progression fields are checked separately even when they alias the same object: default and FirstRun use normal ascension filters, Heat uses the reachable maximum ascension because the game clamps ascension to its cap while Heat is selectable, and missing Heat/FirstRun profiles follow the game's default-profile fallback. In demo mode, full-game-only stages are skipped, but stage indices are not compacted. `scopeToReferencedStages` calculates one matching contiguous range across active progression profiles and rejects profiles with cross-pack use or non-contiguous references. These are static compatibility checks modeled from the pinned game assembly; they do not replace a Steam-launched in-game run.
