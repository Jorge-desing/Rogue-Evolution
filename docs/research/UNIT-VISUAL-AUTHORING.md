All Blender sources for the five detailed designs below keep individually editable parts. Export builders duplicate and merge those parts by material, producing five to eight FBX batches. Blender's local quality checks confirmed ground contact and assigned materials for those five models. The Ironclad uses a reinforced torso, shoulder armor, recessed optics, and paired waist-height Gatling cannons; its 1.83 × 1.98 × 2.45 m bounds fit the approximate `GatlingWalker` envelope. The Unity authoring builder validates its source as a Generic rig with a valid Avatar, bound skinned mesh, and required animation clips; the current manifest declares its visual prefab at `GatlingWalker/RoboMarine_Mesh`. Serialized prefab/bundle inspection shows authored Animator and Avatar/controller references, but does not establish runtime animation compatibility or rendering. This supersedes the historical static-prototype/omitted-overlay description in earlier handoff notes. The Surge Arc Tank adds an insulated generator, twin capacitors, and a forward-facing induction ring centered at the documented LightningTank weapon pivot; its 1.99 × 2.78 × 1.52 m bounds fit the approximate 2.3 × 2.8 × 1.6 m LightningTank envelope. The Colossus retains a long siege cannon that extends beyond the approximate 3.60 m `ArtilleryTank` length; verify spacing and clipping in a Steam-launched match before calling it runtime-ready. The current manifest and bundle also contain Atlas Crane and Bastion Siege mech overlays; they are omitted from this five-model Blender build table because no corresponding repository `tools/Build-*Model.py` builder was found.

## ImageGen card sprites

The Unity authoring project contains seven tracked ImageGen-authored PNGs for the seven units with custom card images; Arachnid and Overlord retain their native cards. Each unit's Blender preview was used to preserve its silhouette; the new Ironclad Walker and Surge Arc Tank images also used the Colossus Siege card for lighting and background style. Their source PNGs are 1254 × 1254: `IroncladWalkerCard.png` SHA-256 `68877148ce681f92a810398d492c456bcb97ef411b35f8d6871e2d1a02a4784a`, and `SurgeArcTankCard.png` SHA-256 `db7283d8c2542a8ec6f0074cdf5e4c41500df2769ec4b1b3fe9677e45455b003`. Unity imports each PNG as a single Sprite with Point filtering, no mipmaps, Clamp wrap mode, Uncompressed texture data, and a 128 × 128 maximum size. The scaffolder contains no procedural card drawing code and fails if a source PNG is missing or does not import as a 128 × 128 Sprite.

## Rebuild and inspect

Run the model builders from the repository root with Blender 4.5.14 LTS:

```powershell
& .\.tools\Blender-4.5.14\blender-4.5.14-windows-x64\blender.exe --background --python tools\Build-SampleScoutModel.py
& .\.tools\Blender-4.5.14\blender-4.5.14-windows-x64\blender.exe --background --python tools\Build-SkirmishStrikeBikeModel.py
& .\.tools\Blender-4.5.14\blender-4.5.14-windows-x64\blender.exe --background --python tools\Build-ColossusSiegeModel.py
& .\.tools\Blender-4.5.14\blender-4.5.14-windows-x64\blender.exe --background --python tools\Build-IroncladWalkerModel.py
& .\.tools\Blender-4.5.14\blender-4.5.14-windows-x64\blender.exe --background --python tools\Build-SurgeArcTankModel.py
```

The Ironclad and Surge Arc Tank builders validate their meshes and bounds, save the editable sources and FBXs, and skip preview rendering so they do not alter existing renders or framing.

The previews go to ignored `artifacts/previews/`. Rebuild the script-free overlays and pack bundle with the pinned Unity Editor:

```powershell
& 'C:\Program Files\Unity\Hub\Editor\2021.3.45f2\Editor\Unity.exe' -batchmode -nographics `
  -projectPath unity\RogueCommandAssetAuthoring `
  -executeMethod RogueCommandExpansion.Authoring.RogueCommandVisualSampleScaffolder.BuildSampleNativeExpansion `
  -quit -logFile artifacts\unity-visual-build-final.log
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe .\tools\validate_content_bundle_references.py .\content\packs\sample-native-expansion
```
